\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{The update.} Let $\theta$ contain the model's $P$ parameters. A generation
(one training update) evaluates $N$ perturbed candidates,
$f_i=f(\theta+\sigma\epsilon_i)$, where $\sigma$ sets the noise scale. It then updates
\begin{equation}
  \Delta\theta = -\frac{\eta}{N\sigma}\sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i\,\epsilon_i ,
  \label{eq:update}
\end{equation}
where $\eta$ is the learning rate and $w_i$ is computed from candidate $i$'s score
\citep{wierstra2014,nesterov2017}. Here a fitness score is a loss to minimize; rewards
enter negated. We use \emph{centered ranks}: scores are ranked and scaled to
$[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]$ \citep{salimans2017}. The update moves toward perturbations
with lower losses and away from those with higher losses. Ranking makes the update
insensitive to strictly increasing transformations of the scores.

\emph{Mirrored} sampling evaluates each perturbation $\epsilon$ together with
$-\epsilon$, so $N$ candidates use $N/2$ independent directions. No gradient is needed
to train. In Section~\ref{sec:quality} we compute a gradient only as a reference and
measure \emph{alignment}, $\cos(\Delta\theta,-\nabla L)$, between an ES update and
the direction that locally decreases a differentiable loss $L$.

\paragraph{Two separate choices.} The perturbation design determines how candidates
are represented and evaluated. The update placement determines where the weighted sum
in Eq.~\ref{eq:update} is computed. We vary both choices independently.

Full-rank Gaussian noise perturbs every weight independently. It can be stored as a
full array for every candidate (\emph{dense}) or regenerated from a seed when needed
(\emph{seed}) \citep{qiu2025}. The latter avoids persistent model-sized noise arrays
per candidate, at the cost of generating the noise for evaluation and again for the update.

Low-rank noise represents the perturbation of an $m\times n$ weight matrix as
$UV^\top/\sqrt r$, where $U$ is $m\times r$ and $V$ is $n\times r$. Rank 1 uses
one column in each factor. The factors store $r(m+n)$ values instead of $mn$, and the
full perturbation matrix need not be formed \citep{eggroll2025}. Each perturbation has
rank at most $r$; the weighted sum over candidates can have higher rank.
Table~\ref{tab:arms} fixes the names used below. Unless qualified, \emph{rank $r$}
means mirrored sampling.
