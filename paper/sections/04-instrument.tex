\section{The library}
\label{sec:instrument}

shardes is built on JAX and its compiler-driven sharding \citep{jax2018,gspmd2021}. It
exposes the usual ask, evaluate and tell loop: \emph{ask} for $N$ perturbed copies of
the model, \emph{evaluate} their fitnesses, \emph{tell} the fitnesses back to get the
update. The population is split across devices and everything else is replicated; for
isotropic ES the distribution state is no larger than the weights, so there is nothing
to gain from splitting it. Three properties make the comparisons in this paper fair.

\paragraph{The device count does not change what is computed.} Member $i$'s seed depends
on $i$ alone, so the population is the same on 1 and on 8 devices. On eight A100s, with
the fitnesses held fixed, the $D{=}1$ and $D{=}8$ updates of Qwen2.5-0.5B agree to
$6.3\times10^{-6}$ relative error, and two 20-generation runs of the same $D{=}8$
program agree byte for byte. Two separately compiled programs need not follow the same
training trajectory: compiler fusion can flip near-tied greedy tokens.

\paragraph{Low-rank noise is never materialized.} A rank-$r$ perturbation stays as its
two factors through evaluation and through the sum (Section~\ref{sec:placement}): a dense layer computes $x(W+\tfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt r}AB^\top)^\top$
as $xW^\top + ((xB)A^\top)\tfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt r}$, so no $m\times n$ noise matrix exists
and all members share the base multiply $xW^\top$. The price is at the API: a model must
route its matrix multiplies and embedding lookups through the library's layers, so an
existing model does not run unmodified.

\paragraph{Fitness is float32 or wider.} In bfloat16, 256 real losses collapsed to 2
distinct values, and ranks computed from ties are noise. The library refuses rather than
casts; the model itself may run in bfloat16.

\paragraph{Gathering fitnesses.} Centered ranks need every fitness, so \emph{tell}
gathers the $N$ scalars to every device and sorts them there. The gather costs
microseconds (Table~\ref{tab:tb8}). The sort is local and repeated on every device: on
the v5e at $N{=}2^{18}$ it takes 12\,ms, the same on 1 and 8 devices. Up to a thousand
members, which covers every experiment here except the largest populations of the cost
grid, the whole ranking step is 60\,$\mu$s or less; at 4096 members it is 0.2\,ms
(Appendix~\ref{sec:barrier}).
